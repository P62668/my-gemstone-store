## Personalized Recommendations

Our AI-powered recommendation system learns from your shopping behavior to suggest products you'll love.

### How It Works
- **Purchase History**: Recommendations are based on your previous purchases
- **Wishlist Items**: Products similar to those in your wishlist
- **Recently Viewed**: Items you've shown interest in
- **Popular Trends**: What other customers are buying

### Where to Find Recommendations
- **Homepage**: Personalized recommendations section
- **Product Pages**: "You May Also Like" section
- **Dedicated Page**: Visit `/recommendations` for all your personalized suggestions

### Benefits
- Discover new products tailored to your taste
- Save time by seeing relevant items first
- Find complementary pieces for your collection

## Enhanced Search

Our smart search feature makes finding exactly what you want faster and easier.

### Features
- **Predictive Search**: Get suggestions as you type
- **Smart Autocomplete**: Search terms are completed based on popular queries
- **Category Filtering**: Search within specific categories
- **Trending Searches**: See what other customers are looking for

### How to Use
1. Start typing in the search bar at the top of any page
2. Browse suggestions that appear below the search bar
3. Click on a suggestion to go directly to that product or category
4. Or press Enter to see all results for your search term

### Search Tips
- Use specific terms like "ruby necklace" or "sapphire earrings"
- Try searching by color, cut, or category
- Check the "Trending Searches" section for inspiration

## Personalized Offers

Exclusive discounts and special offers tailored just for you.

### Types of Offers
- **Loyalty Rewards**: Special discounts based on your loyalty tier
- **Anniversary Bonuses**: Celebrate your membership with us
- **Birthday Specials**: Exclusive offers on your special day
- **Wishlist Deals**: Discounts on items in your wishlist
- **Points Redemption**: Convert loyalty points to discounts

### Where to Find Offers
- **Homepage**: Personalized offers section prominently displayed
- **Account Dashboard**: Visit your account page to see all available offers
- **Email Notifications**: Get notified of new offers via email

### How to Redeem
1. Browse available offers on the homepage or your account page
2. Click "Claim Offer" on any offer you want to use
3. The discount will be automatically applied at checkout
4. Some offers may have expiration dates, so use them soon

## Troubleshooting

### Not Seeing Personalized Content?
1. Make sure you're logged into your account
2. Browse and interact with products to build your profile
3. Check back later as recommendations update regularly

### Offers Not Appearing?
1. Ensure you're logged in
2. Check that you meet any requirements (loyalty tier, etc.)
3. Offers may refresh periodically

### Search Not Working?
1. Try using different keywords
2. Check your internet connection
3. Clear your browser cache if issues persist
